# Interaction proposal: five polish improvements

Status: interactive design fixture; the playable app and engine are unchanged.

Open `eval/interaction-proposal.html`. Select a scenario and use Replay to reset it. Card inspectors use existing card art. The file is self-contained and makes no network calls.

1. **Trustworthy cancellation.** Previewing a target does not create a committed-action receipt. Cancellation names the operation, returns it to HQ and explicitly says no advancement counters were placed. Confirming produces a distinct effect receipt. No unsupported refund promise is made.
2. **Turn and priority.** Turn ownership remains visible while a response tag identifies which player can act. A waiting player sees why the next action is unavailable.
3. **Quieter paid windows.** Continue leads, while every uncommon legal boost remains available in More abilities. Optional boost-only skipping starts off and applies to future eligible windows. Closed windows cannot still be acted in. Engine-side eligibility needs a conservative implementation; this fixture does not establish legality for all cards.
4. **Quoted full break.** Review cost and choose temporary versus pool funding. Boost, opponent response and break remain separate legal steps. Stop retains committed payments. A changed-quote fixture requires fresh confirmation; its cost modifier is synthetic, not a claimed Gateway card interaction. Manual abilities remain available.
5. **Visible reasons.** Used-this-turn, wrong ice type and no unresolved subroutines are distinct reasons. Legal boosts and paid break-zero choices remain available. The availability gallery combines examples from different timing contexts, rather than claiming they occur simultaneously.

The design follows the current app's blue Corp / red Runner table, warm gold actions and decision rail. Mobile review focuses the decision rail; retaining board access on small screens is an explicit review question.
