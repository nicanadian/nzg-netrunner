# Interpreting the expanded suite

Each reviewer records task completion independently from how much the guide helped. A task completed with expert knowledge or builder validation is not evidence that the guide taught the concept.

Interpretation checks:

- Does a Runner win percentage retain its meaning when the player chooses Corp?
- Can the reader explain what the sample size does and does not establish?
- Does an edit preserve the identity minimum, copy limits, faction restrictions, influence and Corp agenda band?
- Does the reader know which functional dependencies are outside that legality check?
- Can a saved revision be found again, distinguished from its baseline, and used without silently changing an existing game's snapshot?
- Does switching pools preserve intent or require restarting navigation/name matching?
- Can the reader name one learning hypothesis, one sacrificed capability and observations that would challenge the hypothesis?
- Is the page usable through keyboard controls and at narrow width, within the available DOM/visual test limits?

Synthesis rules:

Use concrete observed behavior before subjective impressions. Separate independent reviewer agreement from repeated comments by one reviewer. Group distinct symptoms under one root cause without claiming they are the same bug. Treat suggested designs as hypotheses with acceptance criteria, not features tested in this suite. Preserve known task confounds: shared test-profile storage, AI reviewer prior knowledge, scripted routine choices and unavailable screenshots. Do not average reviewer scores across unequal tasks or infer competitive deck strength from the UI games.
