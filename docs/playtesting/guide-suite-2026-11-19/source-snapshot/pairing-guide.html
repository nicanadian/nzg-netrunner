How to read the results

  • 40–60%: close results in this sample—a pairing to try, rather than proof of balance.
  • Below 40%: harder for the Runner in these tests. Above 60%: harder for the Corp.
  • 54 games per pairing: expanded tests for the recommended Vantage decks. Six games: preliminary results for Gateway, Elevation and interaction pilots; one game shifts the percentage by about 17 points.
  • Results describe our AI playing both sides, not human tournament strength. Different lists for the same identity can behave differently.
  • With a friend, swap sides and try a few games before judging a pairing. For learning, familiarity with the cards matters as much as the win record.
  • Custom decks have not been benchmarked here. Use the exact bundled deck names shown above.
