# System Gateway playthrough research

Checked 6 November 2026.

**End-to-end verification:** [All three now have independently replayed, corrected full-game reconstructions](gateway-ledgers/end-to-end-audit.md), totaling 696 legal decisions. Four engine/rules differences were corrected in a versioned profile; original shuffles remain unknown.

**Annotation follow-up:** [All three shortlisted candidates now have timestamped public-action ledgers](gateway-ledgers/README.md): 71 side-turns, 385 events, 45 reconciled Jinteki credit checkpoints, and three passing adapted engine encounter fixtures. Original shuffles and some source discrepancies remain unresolved; these are not three exact executable full-game replays. The inventory below describes the earlier discovery pass, with transcript-access and recording-medium details corrected where noted.

 There is enough public material for a useful human-game evaluation set: four recordings explicitly covering Gateway starter or booster decks, broader narrated matches, two official written teaching sequences, and historical summary statistics covering all twelve pairings among our seven identities. The material is strongest for teaching basic play. Exact Gateway-only faction-deck action traces are much scarcer.

The initial pass inventoried and qualified sources: video titles, creator descriptions, runtimes, chapters and advertised caption tracks. Move-by-move annotation was added later for the three shortlisted recordings, as linked above. The inventory counts below refer to distinct recordings, not verified executable games. BoardGameGeek mirrors, playlists and an unlisted duplicate upload were deduplicated.

The [source catalog](system-gateway-online-sources.json) contains 26 entries, including unavailable leads. The [statistics inspection](system-gateway-stats-summary.json) records the derived counts and data limitations. Game code and the user's saved game were not changed.

Broader videos should supply selected adapted scenarios, not be silently treated as exact recreations.

## Written sequences and transcript access

The official [June 2026 teaching article](https://nullsignal.games/blog/new-learn-to-play-resources/) includes the ordered decks, six scripted side-turns and resource checkpoints corresponding to [the example video we already use](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xtI0-j0utLo). This is the best precise text companion for extending the existing evaluation, although its newer cards require substitutions. It is the same underlying example, not independent match evidence.

An [earlier official teacher script](https://nullsignal.games/l2p-decks-teacher/) has another fixed opening with Zahya and Built to Last. It uses Gateway and Elevation, including Rising Tide and Syailendra. The two script versions must be kept distinct; the older L2P landing page still links this earlier sequence.

All **18 catalogued videos advertise caption tracks** in their public player metadata. Three list English tracks without the ASR marker as well as automatic English tracks; that does not establish transcription accuracy. Most recordings have automatic English captions; the Italian guide advertises Italian captions. Direct caption requests for the Neon Static starter demo and official example returned HTTP 200 with empty bodies. Thus zero new video transcripts were successfully downloaded in the initial discovery pass. In the subsequent reconstruction pass, yt-dlp successfully retrieved English caption tracks for **all three shortlisted videos**. Their narration and video frames now support the [annotated ledgers](gateway-ledgers/README.md); this does not imply the other fifteen videos have been transcribed or reviewed move by move.

Written search results also included podcast transcripts discussing Gateway and short game impressions. Those were excluded from action-sequence counts because they do not enumerate a match's moves.

## Structured historical game data

The public [Jinteki statistics repository](https://github.com/jwarwick/netrunner_stats) contains a [December 2021 CSV](https://github.com/jwarwick/netrunner_stats/blob/main/20211208_games.csv). Inspection of the full file found **111,112 rows**, of which **3,135** have `format == "system-gateway"`, spanning **20 July–8 December 2021**. Its nine columns are `date`, `time`, `turn`, `room`, `format`, `winner`, `reason`, `corp` and `runner`. The units of `time` were not established in this pass. It contains no deck contents or action histories.

Within the Gateway-labelled rows:

- **2,757** record a winner; **378** do not. **3,121** have a turn value.
- **1,798** pair The Catalyst with The Syndicate.
- **807** pair our seven main identities, covering all **12** Runner/Corp combinations. Individual pair counts range from **28** to **125**.
- Some identities are blank, and one row pairs Earth Station with Hoshiko. The format label alone is insufficient proof of deck legality.

These summaries are useful for historical matchup questions after cleaning; interpreting game length also requires checking the `time` and `turn` definitions. They cannot reproduce decisions, reveal which deck variants were played, measure UI confusion, or establish an unbiased balance target. Counts below are records with the indicated identity pair, regardless of whether a winner is filled in.

| Corp / Runner | Loup | Zahya | Tāo |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Precision Design | 50 | 125 | 82 |
| Restoring Humanity | 28 | 51 | 43 |
| Reality Plus | 45 | 85 | 51 |
| Built to Last | 53 | 100 | 94 |

The aggregation is reproducible with Python's `csv.DictReader`: filter the source's `format` column by the exact string `system-gateway`, count nonempty winner/turn fields, and group by the literal `(corp, runner)` pair. The saved statistics JSON contains every pair, including missing and out-of-pool identities; the chart selects only our seven main identities.

## Event records and replay availability

Three organizer-labelled Gateway-only result pages add deck and identity evidence: [Tel Aviv, 8 November 2024](https://alwaysberunning.net/tournaments/4531/tlv-system-gateway-tournament), **9 entrants**; [Kayfabe, 11 March 2025](https://alwaysberunning.net/tournaments/4690/system-gateway-showdown-gnk-kayfabe), **8 entrants**; and [Fargo, 15 March 2025](https://alwaysberunning.net/tournaments/4739/netrunner-local-meetup), **6 entrants**. These are 23 event entries, not necessarily 23 unique people. Their pages list no videos and lack Swiss match data. They are not 23 recoverable game transcripts.

A specifically relevant starter-tournament replay was found via a mirrored community post, but its [direct Jinteki replay URL](https://www.jinteki.net/replay/a76653f6-82d8-42da-ab64-776677d7f14f) returned **404**. No usable Gateway action-replay collection was obtained. The open-source rules engine's test fixtures and our generated AI replays are separate evidence from recorded human play.

## Most useful follow-up for Local Table

1. The three short candidates now have [opening-to-ending ledgers and three adapted encounter fixtures](gateway-ledgers/README.md). The [full-game audit](gateway-ledgers/end-to-end-audit.md) now verifies all three corrected reconstructions. Preserve their source gaps and normalization records when using them as regression references.
2. Use the official June 2026 text companion to strengthen the existing adapted reference. Keep its exact newer-card sequence separate from the Gateway substitutions.
3. Add selected Tāo/Pantograph and other Gateway interactions from the preview stream, checking the surrounding non-Gateway effects before adapting a position.
4. Use the newer narrated series to study how players explain options, payments and consequences. Its decklists make wholesale recreation outside our current card pool.
5. Treat the historical summaries as context for matchup variety. An action-level dataset will still require annotation or volunteered replay exports.

Two sources explicitly publish corrections: the [2022 official rules video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aG0eTf7BncU) corrects a missed strength boost, and [Good Games' example](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DCiugkmct3k) corrects scoring timing, private R&D access and mulligan order. Evaluation should compare intended legal play with the rules, and preserve video mistakes as labelled observations.
