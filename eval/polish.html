What the panel changed

UI / UX

Finding: Ansel could prevent stealing while access instructions still encouraged it. Automatic ice outcomes were hard to follow.

Revision: persistent restriction banners, numbered Broken/Fired states, explicit action costs, contextual hints and phase scroll recovery.

Recheck: verified actual restricted access and automatic resolution in Chrome.

Graphic design

Finding: bright scenery competed with tiny labels; counters, names and pile totals felt like unrelated stickers.

Revision: quieter mats, aligned status strips, clearer labels, consistent badge anchors, clean name rails and purposeful score dialogs.

Recheck: the reviewer found a coherent overall improvement across table, inspector, help and menu.

Board-game design

Finding: “End the run” could be mistaken for the effect of choosing a break target. Scoring and triggered effects lacked clear intent and source.

Revision: numbered “Break subroutine” choices, score-first ordering, READY progress, End action phase, card-source labels and one-based remote names.

Recheck: breaks, bioroid payments, scoring and hidden information passed focused Chrome play.
