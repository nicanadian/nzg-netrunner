Design coverage and rules safeguards
  1. Receipts distinguish cancellation, committed payment and resolved effects. Cancelling a partially paid sequence never claims the earlier payment was refunded.
  2. Turn owner and response priority stay separate. A response window does not become an opponent turn.
  3. All legal abilities remain available. Skipping boost-only windows is optional, off by default and limited to windows with no other available action.
  4. A full-break shortcut follows individual legal abilities. It pauses for responses and rechecks remaining costs; it is not an atomic multi-ability payment.
  5. Disabled reasons stay visible. Legal-but-ineffective actions remain in More abilities, with their consequences explained.
