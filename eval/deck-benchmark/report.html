Method and limits

  • Native Chiriboga AI-vs-AI only; no new Chrome or human playtests in this benchmark.
  • Only two seeds per identity pairing and lineup combination; no claim of tournament strength or human balance.
  • Same seed labels do not imply identical hands because deck sizes and contents differ.
  • Official lists are baselines, not guaranteed optimal lists or current Standard tournament-legal decks.
  • Opening probabilities exclude mulligans, extra draw, accesses and card effects. Taxing/damage ice is distinct from run-stopping ice.
  • Static runner breaker availability excludes tutors, Botulus, click breaks, derez and other ways to access; it is not a run failure probability.

Economy and draw are manually classified roles; conditionally usable cards are not guaranteed opening payouts. Profiles and raw match jobs/results are saved alongside this report. Timing failures remain unresolved; they are never counted as losses.
