Game log

 WELCOME TO NETRUNNER

Two sides. One score.

First to 7 agenda points wins. The Corp also wins if damage empties the Runner’s hand and still has cards to trash. The Runner wins if the Corp must draw from an empty R&D.

As the Runner

Spend your 4 clicks each turn to gain credits, draw, install programs or make runs. Your icebreakers break matching ice subroutines. Reach a server to access cards and steal agendas. Some agendas have additional steal costs.

As the Corporation

Draw at the start of your turn, then spend 3 clicks. Install cards facedown in servers; protect them with ice. Pay to rez your defenses during the Runner’s run. Advance agendas, then score them when they have enough advancement counters.

At this table

Hover or focus cards to magnify them. Click to pin a readable preview and see available actions. The right panel offers every legal decision, including passing a paid ability window. Gold outlines mark cards you can act on. You can undo your last decision from Menu.

Tokens and counters

Bright click markers are available actions; dark, turned-over markers are spent. Credit stacks on cards belong to that card, separate from your credit pool. Their numbers always give the exact total.

Card markers are labeled CR (hosted credits), ADV (advancement), VIR (virus), PWR (power), and AGN (agenda counters). Click a visible card to read its counters and use its available abilities. The engine moves counters for you. Last resource change keeps the net changes between decisions visible, including automatic effects.
